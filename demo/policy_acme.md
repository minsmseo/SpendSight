# Acme Analytics Inc. Expense Policy

Effective January 1, 2026. This policy applies to all employees.

## General
All expenses must be ordinary and necessary for company business. Every expense needs a clear
business purpose. A receipt is required for any expense of $75 or more, and for all hotel stays.
Expenses must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## Pre-approval
Any single purchase above $1,000 requires pre-approval from Finance before the charge is finalized.

## Meals
Client meals are reimbursable up to $250 per meal. List every attendee and their business
relationship. Meals while traveling are reimbursable up to $75 per day. Office snacks and coffee
are reimbursable up to $100 per purchase.

## Travel
Book economy airfare; airfare is reimbursable up to $800 per ticket. Hotel lodging is reimbursable
up to $350 per night. Rideshare and taxi are reimbursable for business travel. Commuting is not reimbursable.

## Entertainment
Client entertainment such as event tickets is reimbursable up to $500 and requires pre-approval above $300.

## Gifts
Business gifts are reimbursable up to $100 per recipient. The recipient name is required.

## Software and supplies
Software subscriptions up to $500 per month do not need approval. Office supplies and accessories
are reimbursable up to $300 per purchase. Splitting a purchase to stay under a limit is prohibited.

## Not reimbursable
Gym memberships, personal clothing, groceries for home, traffic fines and political contributions
are not reimbursable.
